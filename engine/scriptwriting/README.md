# Scriptwriting

Write the words that will be spoken and the words that will be on screen.

**Input:** one approved concept, the analysis it cites, and the chosen template.

**Output:** `script.md`. Spoken lines and supers stay distinct. Each factual line keeps its timecode citation.

The sample script repeats the manifest title card and caption and records that there is no voiceover and no call to action. It adds no product sentences.

The role spec is [the scriptwriter](../../agents/scriptwriter.md).
