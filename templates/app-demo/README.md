# App demo

A walk through a real session. The recording is the picture. Graphics are limited to a title, captions, and callouts for controls that are on screen at that timecode.

## Inputs

- Analysis with at least one complete action (gesture and result), each with timecodes
- Chosen concept from [the brief](../../docs/CONCEPTS_TEMPLATE.md)
- The recording URL

## Outputs

- `script.md` with `VO` optional and `SUPER` lines for the title and captions
- `edit.json` whose picture segments are in-out ranges on that recording
- Master and three platform exports

A cropped still presented as a live flow. A cursor or tap indicator on a control the user never touched. A closing line about results the session did not reach.
