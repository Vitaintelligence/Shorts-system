# Cinematic

Grade, pacing, typography, and music on real captures. The polish is the format. The evidence is still the recording.

## Inputs

- Selects from a recording, listed as timecodes
- A grade note (contrast, crop, speed). Speed changes do not create events that were not filmed
- Music direction: mood and tempo, not a commercial track to copy

## Outputs

- Shot list of selects plus title cards
- EDL with those spans
- Master and platform exports
