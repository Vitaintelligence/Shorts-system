# UGC-style

Creator delivery: to camera, voiceover, or both. The style can be casual. The claims cannot be looser than any other template.

## Inputs

- A real person who will record the delivery, or a decision that the VO is a named narrator and not "a user"
- Analysis timecodes for every claim in their script
- Their recording, stored outside git, once it exists

## Outputs

- The script they will actually read
- A caption list of the lines that appear on screen
- Master and platform exports after their footage exists

## Fill in

- Speaker (name or "narrator", never "satisfied user"):
- Claims they are allowed to say, with timecodes:
- Lines that sound like UGC but are not allowed:
- How the screen recording sits under or beside them:
- Estimated duration:

## Refuse

Fabricated comments, duets, stitches, view counts, or comment screenshots. A synthetic voice labeled as a customer. Slang that smuggles in an uncited benefit.
