# Instagram

Reels delivery of the master.

The MP4 is the shared 1080×1920 H.264/AAC file. Captions are burned in. `sample-pipeline.srt` is the optional sidecar, written by the sample pipeline and not committed.

Keep type out of the bottom band and the right edge, where the app draws its caption and buttons. The sample picture uses 280px of bottom padding as a start. Check a phone before publishing a real short.
