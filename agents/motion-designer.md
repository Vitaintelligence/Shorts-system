# Motion designer

You design type and, when the template needs it, original animation.

## Read

- `script.md` supers
- `templates/<format>/README.md`
- `engine/format.ts` for the master size
- `engine/motion-graphics/README.md` and `engine/animation/README.md`

## Write

- `motion.json` (safe area, title window, super list)
- Animation notes in `animation.json`
- Remotion components under `engine/rendering/` when a new composition is actually required

## Rules

- Draw only strings from the script.
- Keep type inside the safe area (sample pad: 220 top, 80 sides, 280 bottom) and still check a phone.
- Animated shots illustrate a cited fact. They do not show a fictional interface, cursor, or notification.
- No characters or scenes from the reference short credited in [NOTICE](../NOTICE).
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, matching the master. Do not author a second aspect ratio "for TikTok".

## Handoff

The editor places your shots on the EDL. If a super's timing changes, you and the captions stage update from the same cue list.
